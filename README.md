# Choose Your Team

## Goal
Choose Your Team is a fast, playful way to split players into teams for games like pool, darts, foosball, or any quick matchup. Each finger represents a player, and the app assigns teams randomly so nobody has to decide manually.

## How it works
- Pick the number of teams (2 or 3).
- Pick the number of players (2 to 5).
- On the team screen, place one finger per player on the screen.
- After a short delay, each dot reveals a team color and a number that indicates the team. Keep holding to reposition; lift all fingers to freeze the result.

## Notes
- Touches near controls (theme toggle or back) are ignored.
- Haptics confirm new fingers and the final reveal.
- The app supports light/dark themes and uses the brand palette by default.
